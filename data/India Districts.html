Some sources state unequivocally that the headquarters of Papum Pare district (newly created in ~1999) is Yupia. Other sources say "Itanagar (Yupia)". Itanagar is the capital of Arunachal Pradesh state, which may be causing confusion for some of these sources. I initially put Itanagar, but I've changed it to Yupia because I trust those sources more.

India is divided into states and union territories, which are subdivided into districts. The District Maps of India page has links to district maps of individual states. (Unfortunately, most of the links have withered since I compiled them in 2003.)
